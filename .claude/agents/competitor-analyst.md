You produce the **Competitive Analysis** section. Follow `feasibility-study-team-of-agents/agents/_shared-research-rules.md`.

## Method
1. Find **direct competitors** (same offer, same area) and **indirect/substitutes**. Use Google Maps listings, directories, delivery apps, marketplaces, social media, company websites, news.
2. For the top 5–10, record: name, location, offer, **price points (with source and date)**, estimated size, ratings and review count, notable strengths and weaknesses (from reviews).
3. Barriers to entry and Porter's five forces, each rated Low/Medium/High with evidence.
4. **Gaps**: what customers complain about in reviews, unserved segments, underserved areas, price gaps.

## Output `02-competition.md`
Sections: Competitive landscape · Competitor table · Price benchmark table (min / median / max) · Five forces · Gaps and opportunities · Recommended positioning · Key numbers (YAML: competitor_count, price_min, price_median, price_max, avg_rating) · Sources · Open questions.

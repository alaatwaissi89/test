# 05 · Marketing & Sales Plan: Specialty Coffee Kiosk, Amman

**Summary.** The plan positions the unit as **"specialty beans at grab-and-go speed and price"**. The core latte is JOD 2.50, and the blended drink ticket is **JOD 2.45** (GST-inclusive). Pastries are a separate stream at **JOD 1.75 average**, with 0.22 pastries sold per drink. Year 1 sells **40,950 drinks** (ramping from 55 to 155 drinks a day), rising to **82,440 drinks** in year 5. About **15% of drinks** come through Talabat/Careem, at about **25%** commission. Marketing is about **JOD 980 a month** in the launch quarter, **JOD 550 a month** after that (JOD 7,890 in year 1), then **3% of revenue** from year 2.

All prices are GST-inclusive (16%, per 04-legal). Volumes are **drinks**, the unit used in every table.

---

## 1. Positioning

**Statement.** *A specialty-coffee window for people with ten minutes: named single-origin beans, a latte in under three minutes, at JOD 2.50 rather than JOD 2.90–3.00.*

| Gap found in 02-competition | How this plan answers it |
|---|---|
| Specialty quality at a grab-and-go price (JOD 2.25–2.75 gap) | Latte at JOD 2.50. The roaster and origin are named on the menu board and cups. |
| Speed | Pre-order on WhatsApp/Instagram, a pickup shelf, and a short menu of 10 drinks. Target: under 3 minutes at peak (ASSUMPTION, to be checked by operations). |
| Office batch boxes | A 12-drink office box, ordered by WhatsApp and delivered by hand, with no app commission. |
| Monthly passes | 20-drink core pass and a student americano pass, aimed at repeat visits. |

**Brand tone (Assessment):** local and independent. This matters in Amman, where boycotts of US chains changed footfall in 2024 ([NPR/NHPR, Mar 2024](https://www.nhpr.org/2024-03-13/in-jordan-american-companies-see-boycotts-sparked-by-the-u-s-s-support-for-israel), cited in 01-market). Café culture among young people is a growing social habit ([Friedrich Naumann Foundation, Dec 2025](https://www.freiheit.org/jordan/youth-choice-public-life-ammans-cafe-boom)).

**Licensing constraint on marketing.** The unit is a **licensable small shop unit**, not a street kiosk. GAM refuses new licences for coffee sold to drivers and pedestrians on the street ([GAM](https://media.ammancity.gov.jo/En/NewsDetails/GAM__No_New_licenses_for_cafeterias_Selling_Coffee_for_Drivers_and_Pedestrians)). Marketing must therefore never promote kerbside or "drive-by" service. Pickup happens at the shop counter. Confidence: High.

## 2. Pricing

Benchmarks from 02-competition: core milk drink min JOD 2.00 (Qahwa BLK), median 2.90 (Starbucks), max 3.00 (Dimitri's). Americano JOD 1.25–2.60. Spanish latte 2.50–4.20. Brewed/cold brew 3.00–3.74. Croissant 1.95–2.97. Cookie 1.00–2.50.

| Product | Price (JOD) | Benchmark position | Assumed share of drinks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Espresso / americano (hot or iced) | 1.75 | between Qahwa BLK 1.25 and Starbucks 2.60 | 25% |
| Latte / cappuccino / flat white | **2.50** | below the 2.90 median, above the 2.00 minimum | 35% |
| Spanish latte / flavoured latte | 3.00 | lower end of the 2.50–4.20 range | 20% |
| Cold brew / iced specialty | 2.75 | below Starbucks cold brew at 3.30 | 12% |
| Single-origin V60 / filter | 3.25 | lower half of the 3.00–3.74 range | 8% |
| **List-price weighted average** | **2.50** | (0.25×1.75 + 0.35×2.50 + 0.20×3.00 + 0.12×2.75 + 0.08×3.25) | 100% |
| Core pass: 20 drinks (americano/latte/cappuccino) | 40.00 (2.00/drink) | local benchmark: 30 americanos for JOD 30 ([@loganajo, Aug 2026](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Db3GWJdMfwK/)) | inside the mix |
| Student americano pass: 20 drinks | 30.00 (1.50/drink) | same benchmark | inside the mix |
| Office box: 12 drinks | 26.00 (2.17/drink) | Qahwa BLK 12 coffees + fatayer for JOD 25 ([Talabat](https://www.talabat.com/jordan/restaurant/638332/qahwa-blk-abdoun?aid=4908)) | inside the mix |
| Pastry (cookie 1.50, croissant 2.00, fatayer 1.50) | **1.75 avg** | inside the croissant (1.95–2.97) and cookie (1.00–2.50) ranges | separate stream |

**Blended average ticket per drink: JOD 2.45.** ASSUMPTION, derived as follows: 88% of drinks sell at the JOD 2.50 list average and 12% go through passes and boxes at about JOD 2.10. That gives 0.88×2.50 + 0.12×2.10 = **2.45**. Delivery-app prices match walk-up prices, so the commission is a cost and not a price markup. Confidence: Medium. The ticket is below 01-market's JOD 2.75 average spend because the positioning targets the gap under the median. The finance agent should stress-test it at 2.20 and 2.75.

**Pastry stream.** 01-market assumes pastries in 25% of orders. With about 1.15 drinks per order (ASSUMPTION: some orders are for two people), that is **0.22 pastries per drink**. The price is **JOD 1.75**, not the 1.50 placeholder, because the croissant and cookie benchmarks above support it. Confidence: Low–Medium.

**Effective delivery cost:** 15% of drinks × 25% = about **3.75% of total drink revenue**.

**Assessment.** Instagram is the main paid channel for students and young professionals. Influencers come only at launch and once a month after that. Delivery apps are worth listing on for reach, but promotions stay off them, because each app sale gives up a quarter of its price.

## 4. Launch plan (first 90 days) and budget

| Period | Actions | Cost (JOD) |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-opening (weeks −4 to 0) | Brand identity, menu board and cup stamp design (ASSUMPTION 500). Instagram account seeded with roaster and origin content. Sign-ups for Talabat/Careem (the vocational licence is needed first, per 04-legal). | 500 one-off |
| Opening week | 300 free tasting drinks at about JOD 0.70 ingredient cost each (ASSUMPTION; replace with the operations COGS) = 210. Five micro and five nano creators invited. | 210 one-off |
| Months 1–3 | Meta ads JOD 450/month (inside both agencies' ranges; ~110–250k impressions at JOD 1.80–4.00 CPM). Influencers: 5 micro × 100 + 5 nano × 50 = 750, +20% headroom = 900 over 3 months (≈300/month). Office sampling and flyers 150/month (ASSUMPTION). App-funded discounts 80/month (ASSUMPTION). | **980/month** |
| Months 4–12 | Meta 300/month. One micro creator a month at 100. Sampling 50/month. App promotions 100/month. | **550/month** |
| Year 2 onward | **3% of gross revenue** (ASSUMPTION; about JOD 415/month in year 2, which still covers the Meta minimum of 250–400). | ~5,000/yr |

**Year-1 marketing:** 3×980 + 9×550 = **JOD 7,890** (JOD 658/month average), plus JOD 710 pre-opening. That is about 6.8% of year-1 gross revenue.

**CAC check (Assessment, Low confidence).** Suppose 70% of drinks come from regulars buying about 80 drinks a year (01-market). Then year 1 builds roughly 40,950×0.7/80 ≈ **360 regulars**. Year-1 marketing divided by regulars gives a blended **CAC of about JOD 22**, against roughly JOD 196 of drink revenue per regular per year (80 × 2.45). Payback is short even at a 30% gross margin.

## 5. Sales forecast

**Method.** Volumes are built from 01-market's SOM (117 / 163 / 210 drinks a day in years 1–3) on 30 trading days a month (360/yr). No business opens at full volume. The ramp starts at about 47% of the year-1 SOM rate in month 1, reaches it by month 6–7, and exits year 1 at 155 a day, close to the year-2 level. The year-1 average (114/day) is slightly below SOM, which is deliberately conservative. **Capacity check:** 210 drinks a day is about 15/hour over 14 hours, and peaks of 40–60/hour are within what a 2-group espresso machine with 2 baristas can do (ASSUMPTION, operations to confirm). Years 4–5 add +5% and +4% volume growth, below the 6% nominal CAGR, because a single site's catchment saturates.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Delivery contract.** Ask Talabat and Careem for written commission quotes. Our 25% is the top of a 15–25% range, and new merchants can sometimes negotiate launch discounts.
2. **App pricing.** Will you charge the same price on the apps (as modelled) or add about 10% to recover part of the commission?
3. **Roaster.** Which local roaster will be named on the menu? The "named beans" positioning depends on it.
4. **Pass appetite.** Are you comfortable with JOD 1.50–2.00 per drink on passes? If more than 12% of drinks go through passes, the JOD 2.45 ticket falls.
5. **Office contacts.** Can you name 5–10 offices within 500 m to sample in month 1?
6. **Seasonality.** Will you open during Ramadan daytime and the summer break? The ramp assumes 30 trading days every month.
7. **Creative.** Who runs Instagram day to day: you, the barista, or a freelancer? A freelancer is not in this budget.

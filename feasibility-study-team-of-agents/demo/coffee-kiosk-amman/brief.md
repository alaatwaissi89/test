# Project Brief (demo)

- **Concept:** Specialty coffee takeaway kiosk (espresso drinks, cold brew, pastries from a supplier), about 15–25 m², walk-up and delivery-app orders.
- **Location:** Amman, Jordan, a busy mixed office/university area (ASSUMPTION: e.g. Abdoun, Sweifieh, Khalda or near University of Jordan; the operations agent picks the best candidate with evidence).
- **Target customers:** office workers, university students, young professionals.
- **Scale:** small, one kiosk, owner-managed.
- **Currency:** JOD.
- **Capital:** ASSUMPTION: up to about JOD 40,000, equity funded, no loan.
- **Timeline:** open within 4–6 months.
- **Owner experience:** ASSUMPTION: no prior café experience; hires an experienced barista.
- **Purpose:** demo run of the agent team so the owner can judge quality.

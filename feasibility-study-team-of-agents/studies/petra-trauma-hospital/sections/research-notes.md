## Licensing (owner-supplied guide "الدليل التنفيذي لترخيص وإنشاء وتشغيل مستشفى خاص في الأردن", v1.0, 26 Sep 2026; cites نظام المستشفيات الخاصة 54/2014 + معدل 63/2022, Official Gazette 5825, 16/11/2022)
- Hospital types: GENERAL = min 40 beds incl. surgery, OB/GYN, internal medicine, paediatrics; SPECIALISED = min 20 beds; dental = 10 (p.3). => 30-bed trauma/ortho must be licensed as a SPECIALISED hospital (مستشفى متخصص), or grow to 40+ with the four departments to be general.
- MoH preliminary approval valid 5 years; lapses automatically if construction not complete. Operating forbidden before final approval + minister's start-of-work permit.
- Fees: first licence JOD 100/bed; renewal JOD 75/bed every 5 years (25% discount in some cases); in-hospital pharmacy 2,000; lab 100; cath lab/lithotripsy/endoscopy units 1,000 each (p.10).
- ORs: min 2 + prep + recovery + 2 changing rooms; height 3.50 m structural/3.00 m clear. Corridors 2.40 m. Water tank ≥250 m³; fuel ≥2 weeks. Parking: 1 per bed + 1 per 100 m² GFA (free). ICU: general 4 beds; specialised 2 beds + 1 isolation. Ambulances: 2 up to 100 beds (one available 24/7). Morgue: 1 fridge per 50 beds. Rooms: suite 20 m², single 12 m², double 16 m², 3rd class 8 m²/bed. Biomedical engineer required >100 beds. EMR required for every inpatient (deadline 16/11/2027 for paper conversion). Staff lists to MoH every 6 months.
- Staffing nationality: Jordanian doctors ≥90% of doctors; non-doctor staff ≥80% Jordanian; ≥75% of interns/residents Jordanian. GM must be a licensed physician (p.24-25).
- Critical: do not buy land or freeze design before a coordination meeting with MoH Directorate of Licensing of Professions and Health Institutions; zoning/use letter needed before purchase (p.3, p.29-30).
- Reference timeline (gates): 0–3 m scope/site; 2–6 m concept + MoH request; 5–12 m detailed design, civil defence, environment, radiation; 10–30 m construction; 24–34 m commissioning; 30–36 m occupancy & sub-licences; 34–38 m MoH inspection, final licence, start permit (p.31). => ~3 years to opening.
- Radiation: EMRC institutional licence for CT/X-ray/C-arm; shielding report before construction. Environment: classification decision from Ministry of Environment (Reg. 69/2020). Medical waste instructions 2024 replaced 2001.
